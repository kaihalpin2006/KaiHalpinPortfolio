About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at UC Irvine with a background in mechanical design, mechatronics, and rapid prototyping. I use SolidWorks, 3D printing, and laser cutting to move quickly from concept to working hardware, and I machine parts on manual lathes and mills. I also lead the electrical and mechanical team on UCI's SEDS Mars rover.

Mechanical Hammer Design

Machining coursework

Designed in SolidWorks and machined from raw stock on a manual lathe, vertical mill, and horizontal/vertical bandsaw, finished with calipers and a micrometer.

Functional hammer within ±0.010 in. tolerance.

Pneumatic Rack & Pinion Robot

Class robotics competition

Autonomous pneumatic robot for an obstacle course. Designed the electrical schematics, soldered and integrated all subsystems, and fabricated parts by laser cutting, 3D printing, and drill press. A PID controller with magnetometer feedback handles heading correction.

1st place for overall robot performance.

Space Exploration Rover

SEDS UCI · Electrical & Mechanical Team Lead

Leading a team of 4+ on a rover that locates a payload, drives to it autonomously, lifts it with an actuated bucket, and returns to base. Developed the electrical diagram and sourced and wired batteries, motors, cameras, IR sensors, and servos.

Autonomous navigation to payloads using IR sensors and camera feedback.

Featherweight Modular BattleBot

ACE Robotics

1 lb BattleBot with interchangeable horizontal and drum spinner weapons. Contributed to the SolidWorks CAD, 3D printed components, and routed and soldered most of the electrical system.

Top 4 at ACE Antweight Assault.

Vertical Wall Climber

Montera

Climbing device designed to hold a wall for a 2-minute runtime and mount and dismount easily. Helped design the module in Fusion 360, soldered motor connections, and wired a Raspberry Pi and H-bridge stepper.

Stuck to the wall for extended periods and moved with a remote steering device.

Movement Modules

Montera

Modules that move, locate, and attach to sister modules. Worked on the electrical side: chose the battery and motors, soldered all 4 motors, and paired modules to a remote.

Modules located each other and moved in all directions across terrain.

Bicycle CAD Assembly

SolidWorks

Modeled my own bicycle using reference geometry, 3D sketches, sweeps, lofts, and tangent planes, including a lofted ergonomic seat.

Fully constrained 23-part, 4-subassembly model validated with FEA.

Drone Project

PeterWorks ASME

Modeled the airframe in SolidWorks, 3D printed and laser cut structural parts, and helped choose motors, propellers, ESCs, and battery to balance thrust, endurance, and weight.

Successfully flew in the PeterWorks Drone Competition.

Well Injection Analysis

Sentinel Peak Resources

Built a database from injection profile reports using Power Automate Desktop and Excel to find wells injecting too little or too much water.

Flagged 1,000+ intervals in 193 under-injecting wells and 103 intervals in 62 over-injecting wells.

Image Classification Model

MSEIP-STALP internship

Built a convolutional neural network in Python with Keras and TensorFlow to tell cats from dogs, trained on 20,000+ images.

About 91% accuracy after 10 epochs; presented to 25+ supervisors and students.
